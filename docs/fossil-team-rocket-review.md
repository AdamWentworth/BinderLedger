## Separate Exception Printings

PriceCharting exposes several products that must not be merged into the normal
set cards. They belong in a future exception-variant pass with their own images
and prices:

- Fossil Aerodactyl 01/62 prerelease variants.
- Fossil Haunter 06/62 cigar, incomplete-holo, and stain errors.
- Fossil Krabby 51/62 error.
- Team Rocket Dark Dragonite error variants.
- Team Rocket Dark Gyarados 08/82 prerelease.
- Team Rocket The Boss's Way 73/82 First Edition thick-stamp error.

The standard Unlimited image collection intentionally excluded all of these.
The regular Unlimited Dark Dragonite holo is PriceCharting's `[Holo]` product,
TCGplayer ID 84584. Its plain `Dark Dragonite #5` product is the missing-holo
error, TCGplayer ID 84586, and must never be used as the regular holo source.
